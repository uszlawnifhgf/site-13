Our Corporate Foundation

Delivering precision back-office solutions for innovative enterprises.

Located in the historic city of Osnabrück, Kai Haferkamp Toys & Games Verwaltungs GmbH is a specialized administrative and business management firm. Under the experienced leadership of Key Principal Kai Haferkamp, our company provides tailored administrative support to businesses within the dynamic toys and games sector. We understand that creative industries require robust, highly organized back-office infrastructure to truly thrive in competitive global markets. Our dedicated professionals handle complex administrative tasks, allowing creators and game developers to focus entirely on their innovative products. By implementing efficient management protocols and streamlined operational workflows, we ensure our clients' businesses run with absolute German precision. Ultimately, our mission is to deliver the reliable administrative foundation that helps toy and game companies achieve sustainable commercial success.
